# Changelog

## 2026-11-25

- First release: Hinqoc edition based on the Lilpa pinball build, with Hinqoc's 12 subscription personacons as marbles
  (one per name, drawn without smoothing, art at 0.85x of the ball size), a Hinqoc name tag above the minimap,
  and the "Hinqoc night sky" panel colors. Personacons are stored encrypted in the repository and decrypted at build time.
